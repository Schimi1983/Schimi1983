### Hi there 👋

I'm into **smart home and energy** – mostly around [ioBroker](https://www.iobroker.net/) and [evcc](https://evcc.io/): PV, home batteries, EV charging and everything that helps to use your own energy more cleverly.

I like to build, fix and maintain adapters and integrations for devices I use myself – so I can test them in real life, not just on paper. Contributions to existing open source projects are part of that too.

Everything I publish is free to use. Bug reports, ideas and pull requests are always welcome.
